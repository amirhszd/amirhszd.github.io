I am a Research Assistant Professor at Rochester Institute of Technology, working at the intersection of remote sensing, machine learning, and physics-based simulation. I develop methods and software for extracting meaningful information from satellite, UAV, hyperspectral, thermal, and LiDAR observations.

My current research spans self-supervised hyperspectral representation learning, Landsat surface-temperature retrieval and uncertainty, and large-scale DIRSIG scene construction.

I earned my Ph.D. in Imaging Science at RIT in 2022. My doctoral work connected greenhouse spectroscopy with field-scale UAV observations for crop-yield prediction and harvest-maturity assessment.

I teach Applications of Machine Learning in Remote Sensing and advise graduate and undergraduate researchers.
