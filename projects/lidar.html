3D Point Cloud Voxel Content Estimation in Simulated Forests

Jan 2023 – Jan 2025
Read paper ↗

IEEE Transactions on Geoscience and Remote Sensing, 2026.

Overview

3D LiDAR Voxel Content Estimation is a deep-learning framework for estimating the percentage of bark, leaf, soil, and manmade material within individual forest voxels using only 3D coordinates and LiDAR intensity. The architecture combines Kernel Point Convolutions (KPConv) with an encoder–decoder structure and skip connections to capture spatial context and perform multi-target regression of material composition. Using LiDAR data simulated in DIRSIG for Harvard Forest, the research investigates how limited observational information can reveal mixed material content within voxels while addressing imbalanced material distributions.

Contribution

  • Estimated bark, leaf, soil, and other fractions from voxelized LiDAR.
  • Developed an imbalance-aware point-cloud deep learning model.
  • Benchmarked against state-of-the-art deep learning models.
  • Performed sensitivity and ablation studies.
