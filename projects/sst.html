Temporal Hyperspectral Representation Learning for Biotechnology

Self-supervised temporal hyperspectral representation learning for biotechnology.

March 2026 – PresentOngoing research

Overview

Temporal Hyperspectral Representation Learning for Biotechnology is a self-supervised framework for learning robust representations from multi-temporal hyperspectral imagery. The framework combines a masked autoencoder with factorized spectral, spatial, and temporal attention, FiLM-based metadata conditioning, and adversarial domain prediction to reduce the influence of collection geometry and acquisition effects in the latent space. Data-processing pipelines span greenhouse, UAS, and EO-1 Hyperion satellite observations, including the 4.5 TB ChronoEarth dataset of approximately 500,000 image chips across up to 235 timestamps over 17 years. The research investigates whether latent-space trajectories can distinguish healthy from stressed vegetation while suppressing observational and environmental variability; representation quality is evaluated through downstream tasks such as segmentation.
